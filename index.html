About me

I like solving problems that matter in the real world.

My interest in industrial engineering has grown from seeing the same ideas appear in very different environments: a fulfillment center, a building commissioning project, a classroom, and a community organization can all be viewed as systems with people, constraints, information, and opportunities for improvement.

I am especially interested in operations, process improvement, automation, statistics, and project leadership. I enjoy moving between the analytical side of a problem and the people side of actually making a solution work.

May–July 2026

Amazon — Area Manager Intern

I directed daily operations for an outbound fulfillment area of 110–130 associates while leading three concurrent continuous improvement projects involving dock automation, trailer security compliance, and defect reduction. Across these projects, I learned to connect operational data to decisions that affect people, productivity, safety, and cost.

What I learned: A technically good solution is not enough. Process improvement requires understanding the operation, communicating with stakeholders, and making changes practical for the people who use the process.

KSAs applied: Leadership, project management, continuous improvement (Lean), operational data analysis, stakeholder communication, conflict resolution, and people management skills.

Reflection on growth: During a midpoint review I misunderstood an assignment; I sought mentor feedback, revised my approach, and produced a more well-rounded final project. I also practiced delivering difficult feedback and improved my body language and framing for more productive conversations.
